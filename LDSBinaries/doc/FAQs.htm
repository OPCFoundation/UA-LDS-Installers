OPC UA Local Discovery Server 1.02

Who can use the LDS; Can I deploy it to my customers?
Yes, the LDS is actually free to distribute to anybody, including non-members. Vendors can include the LDS merge module as part of their installation, and end-users can download the installer directly from the OPC Foundation website.

What platforms is the LDS available for?
The OPC Foundation provides the LDS for Windows and Linux.

Is the source-code available for the LDS?
Yes, to OPC Foundation Corporate Members.

Who maintains the LDS?
The OPC Foundation maintains the LDS. The actual development is contracted.

Can I modify the LDS endpoints to allow insecure connections?
Yes, simply modify the configuration file and then restart the LDS.

Who do I contact for help?
Use the Forum for general questions and assistance, or use Mantis to report problems.
